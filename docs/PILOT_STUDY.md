# Pilot Study — Ready Version

**Status:** Ready to run with 30 genuine student volunteers.

## Design
- Pre-test: 10 app items, 2 sample loan-cost estimates, confidence rating.
- Intervention: 15–20 minutes using Safe Loan Check.
- Post-test: a different 10-item set, 2 different sample loan-cost estimates, confidence rating.
- Usability: 1–5 rating plus a short open comment.

## Measures
- Risk-classification accuracy before and after.
- Cost-estimate accuracy before and after.
- Confidence before and after.
- Usability score.
- Common points of confusion.

## Privacy
Use anonymous Participant IDs (P01–P30). Do not collect names, phone numbers, bank details, Aadhaar details, or personal loan amounts.

## Reporting rule
Use genuine participant data only. Do not add synthetic participant rows. Report weak, zero, or negative findings honestly.

See `pilot/Safe_Loan_Check_Pilot_Study_Pack.docx` and `pilot/Safe_Loan_Check_Pilot_Scoring_Tracker.xlsx`.
