# Safe Loan Check — Campus Survey

Keep this survey anonymous. Do not collect names, phone numbers, account numbers, or actual loan amounts.

1. Have you ever seen advertisements for instant loan apps? (Yes / No)
2. Have you or someone you know used an instant loan app? (Yes / No / Prefer not to say)
3. What would you most likely borrow for? (Fees / Travel / Phone / Emergency / Other)
4. Before borrowing, how would you check whether an app is safe? (Open answer)
5. Have you heard of RBI's Digital Lending Apps Directory? (Yes / No)
6. Do you know what APR or a Key Fact Statement means? (Yes / Somewhat / No)
7. Would a free tool that checks loan apps be useful to you? (1–5 scale)
8. Have you heard of anyone receiving threatening calls from a loan app? (Yes / No / Prefer not to say)

Target: at least 100 responses for the project evidence plan.
