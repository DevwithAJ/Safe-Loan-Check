
# Safe Loan Check — Google Form Ready Survey

Use this form anonymously. Do not ask for name, phone number, Aadhaar, bank details, account number, or actual loan amount.

## Form title
**Safe Loan Check — Digital Loan App Awareness Survey**

## Description
We are conducting an academic survey to understand awareness of digital lending apps among students/young users. Responses are anonymous and will be used only for educational project research. Please do not enter any personal financial information.

## Questions

1. **Have you ever seen advertisements for instant loan apps?**

   - No

2. **Have you or someone you know used an instant loan app?**

   - Travel
   - Phone / device
